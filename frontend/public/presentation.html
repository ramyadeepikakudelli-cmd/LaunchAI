Engineering Depth

Generative AI Pipeline & Key Innovations

🧠

1. Prompt Denoising

Natural language prompts are mapped into latent space representations and synthesized via multi-step latent diffusion into brand-new 768x768 / 1024x1024 visuals in ~6 seconds.

🛡️

2. Anti-CORS Local Cache

To prevent the browser's "Tainted Canvas" security error on export, the FastAPI backend downloads the visual and stores it in `./storage/generated/`, serving it from the same origin.

⚡

3. Single-Slot Architecture

Eliminated concurrent multi-image requests that caused IP throttling. Generates 1 high-fidelity visual with an instant "Re-roll" capability for unlimited creative iteration.

Regulatory Governance

TRAI Compliance Audit Engine

How LAUNCH AI protects telecoms from multimillion-rupee regulatory violations:

Rule 01

Plan Name & Category

Verifies clear classification (e.g. "5G Prepaid") without deceptive promotional naming.

Rule 02

Price Transparency

Ensures tariffs explicitly mention whether 18% GST is included or extra to prevent hidden fees.

Rule 03

FUP Limit Disclosures

Enforces daily quota and reduced post-FUP speed (e.g., 64 Kbps) disclosures under TRAI mandates.

Rule 04

Validity Duration

Mandates exact billing cycle disclosure (28 days vs 30 days monthly billing transparency).

Rule 05

Asterisk Disclaimer Presence

Audits legibility and font contrast of the mandatory fine-print asterisk disclaimer footer.

Rule 06

Aspect Ratio Dimensions

Validates that canvas dimensions match approved channel ratios without squashing or distortion.
